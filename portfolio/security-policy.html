🛡️ Security Policy

⚠️ RESPONSIBLE DISCLOSURE

If you discover a potential security vulnerability in this portfolio website, please report it responsibly using the contact information and guidelines provided below.

This policy is intended to provide a clear process for responsible security reporting regardless of where the researcher is located. It is not a bug-bounty program and does not provide compensation for vulnerability reports.

🔐 Reporting Security Vulnerabilities

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability affecting this portfolio website, please report it as soon as reasonably possible.

Contact: rutvijbhatt207@gmail.com

Preferred Language: English

Response: Reports will be reviewed as reasonably practicable. No specific response or resolution time is guaranteed.

📋 Responsible Disclosure Guidelines

When investigating and reporting a potential vulnerability, please:

  • Act in good faith and avoid unnecessary disruption to the website or its users.
  • Test only the systems, pages, resources, and functionality that are within the scope of this policy.
  • Do not access, modify, delete, download, disclose, or retain data that does not belong to you.
  • Do not intentionally degrade, interrupt, overload, or otherwise disrupt the availability or performance of the website.
  • Do not use social engineering, phishing, physical attacks, credential theft, or attacks against other individuals.
  • Avoid automated testing or scanning that could negatively affect the website's availability or hosting infrastructure.
  • Do not publicly disclose the vulnerability before providing a reasonable opportunity for it to be reviewed and addressed.
  • Provide sufficient technical information to allow the issue to be understood and, where reasonably possible, reproduced.

📝 What to Include in a Report

To help with investigation, please include as much of the following information as reasonably possible:

  • Description of the suspected vulnerability
  • Affected URL, page, or functionality
  • Steps required to reproduce the issue
  • Expected behavior
  • Actual behavior
  • Potential security impact
  • Relevant screenshots, logs, or proof-of-concept information
  • Browser, operating system, or relevant testing environment

Please avoid including passwords, authentication tokens, personal information, or other sensitive information in a vulnerability report unless it is strictly necessary to demonstrate the issue.

🌐 Scope

This policy applies to the following portfolio website and resources that are directly controlled by or operated for the portfolio:

  • Main portfolio website:
    https://rutvij01.github.io/Live/portfolio/
  • Associated pages, files, and resources that are part of the portfolio website and are directly controlled or maintained by the website owner.

🚫 Out of Scope

The following activities and systems are outside the scope of this security policy:

  • Third-party websites, applications, APIs, services, or infrastructure that are not directly controlled by Rutvij Bhatt.
  • GitHub's infrastructure, GitHub accounts, or GitHub services themselves.
  • Social engineering, phishing, or attacks against people.
  • Physical attacks or attempts to gain unauthorized physical access.
  • Denial-of-service or distributed denial-of-service testing.
  • Spam, automated abuse, or activities intended to degrade availability.
  • Vulnerabilities that exist entirely within third-party software, services, libraries, hosting infrastructure, or dependencies and are not caused by the portfolio website's own implementation.

🛡️ Good-Faith Testing

Good-faith security research is welcome when performed responsibly and within the scope of this policy.

If you discover a potential vulnerability and follow these guidelines, please report the issue before taking further action that could increase risk to the website, its users, or its data.

Where appropriate and permitted by applicable law, good-faith vulnerability research that follows this policy will be considered when evaluating whether further action is necessary.

This policy does not grant permission to access systems or data that are outside the stated scope, nor does it override applicable laws, regulations, third-party terms, or hosting-provider policies.

Nothing in this policy creates a contractual promise to waive, release, or limit any legal rights or remedies that may apply to security testing activities.

⏱️ Disclosure and Coordination

Please provide a reasonable opportunity for the issue to be reviewed and addressed before publicly disclosing technical details.

The appropriate disclosure timeline may depend on the severity and complexity of the vulnerability.

If coordinated disclosure is appropriate, the researcher and website owner may communicate regarding the status and potential disclosure timeline.

🏆 Recognition

Security researchers who responsibly disclose valid vulnerabilities may be acknowledged on this page or another appropriate location, only with their prior permission.

No monetary compensation, reward, employment opportunity, or other benefit is guaranteed for submitting a security report.
